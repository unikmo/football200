# Football200 Legal Release Gate

Production release is BLOCKED until all items below are terminal and backed by real documents/evidence.

## Required legal inputs

- Verified operator identity for the Impressum; do not invent names, addresses, registration or tax identifiers.
- Qualified review/final text for privacy and data-protection disclosures based on the final production architecture.
- Qualified review/final participation terms covering minors, selection, Community Fan Pass and Family Plus.
- Qualified review/final sponsorship terms, including invoicing/VAT/payment treatment and publication rights.
- Qualified review/final club agreement, including the 30% reinvestment commitment and operational roles.
- Qualified review of the school-distribution model and respective data-protection/safeguarding responsibilities.
- Qualified review of parent/guardian confirmation and granular photo/story/social/sponsor-sharing consent wording and withdrawal process.
- Qualified review before using a sponsor-supported `Halbzeit-Verlosung` or similar promotion.

## Technical gates tied to legal approval

Real child applications, real guardian confirmations and real media consents remain disabled while the legal gate is open. The staging implementations accept only explicit synthetic test data.

**REQUIRES QUALIFIED LEGAL REVIEW before production.**
